Pre-med milestones

  1. Fall 2026 (now)

    Register with PreMed and PreHealth Advising and create your Medical Applicant Portal (MAP) profile. Book meetings with your Khoury advisor about the items in the checklist below.

  2. Spring 2027

    Start steady clinical volunteering in the Bay Area. Consistency over time matters more than total hours.

  3. Fall 2027

    Arrive in Boston. Look for a research lab (computational biology is a natural fit) and start shadowing physicians.

  4. Fall 2028

    Co-op search. Target clinical research, hospital data science, or health-tech roles so the co-op doubles as medical-school experience.

  5. July–September 2029

    MCAT study and test date. All the science it covers (both chemistries, physics, biochemistry, psychology, sociology) is finished by December 2028.

  6. Fall 2029 – Spring 2030

    Collect recommendation letters and complete Northeastern's committee letter process. Write your personal statement.

  7. June 2030

    Graduate and submit your primary application early in the cycle. Gap year while interviewing, matriculating in fall 2031.
